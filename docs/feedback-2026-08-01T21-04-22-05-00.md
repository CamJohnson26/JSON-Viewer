## Interpretation Notes

- "Clicking on a boolean makes it turn lower case" is treated as a click/presentation regression. The requested double-click behavior needs a focused interaction test before deciding whether double-click edits or toggles.
- "Nested Adds are broken" and "Add button disappears" are treated as defects, not visual preferences.
- "Don't need the rename, duplicate, etc features" may mean removing always-visible row controls rather than deleting the underlying commands. Human confirmation is required before reducing shipped capability.
- Empty array/object selection and replace-by-default paste conflict with the current approved inference and clipboard contracts, so neither should be changed implicitly.
